# Enterprise Customer Support & Service Operations Policy

## § 1. Service Level Agreements (SLAs) & Response Windows
Our support operations maintain four priority tiers based on customer plan and issue severity:
- **Critical Severity (P1):** Complete system outages affecting core functionality. Initial response window is **15 minutes** for Enterprise customers and **1 hour** for Professional plans. Resolution target is under 4 hours.
- **High Severity (P2):** Significant impairment with no functional workaround. Initial response window is **2 business hours**.
- **Standard Severity (P3):** General defects, minor workflow impairments, and billing inquiries. Initial response window is **12 business hours**.
- **Low Severity (P4):** General product questions, feature requests, and documentation clarifications. Initial response window is **24 business hours**.
Support channels operate 24/7/365 for Critical issues via emergency phone hotline, and Monday through Friday 08:00 to 20:00 EST for Standard inquiries.

## § 2. Refund & Cancellation Terms
- **30-Day Money-Back Guarantee:** First-time annual software subscribers are eligible for a 100% full refund within 30 days of the original purchase timestamp.
- **Monthly Subscription Refunds:** Monthly subscription charges are non-refundable once the billing cycle begins; however, users may cancel anytime to prevent renewal on the subsequent billing date.
- **Exceptions for Unresolved Outages:** If a paid enterprise tenant experiences documented platform downtime exceeding SLA thresholds (greater than 99.9% uptime SLA breach) during a calendar month, a 15% service credit or prorated refund is automatically granted upon ticket review.
- **Processing Timeline:** Approved refunds are initiated back to the original payment instrument within **5 to 7 business days**.

## § 3. Warranty Coverage & Hardware Replacements
- **Standard 1-Year Limited Warranty:** All physical terminal hardware, barcode readers, and IoT telemetry sensors manufactured by our partner network are warranted against defects in materials and craftsmanship for exactly 365 calendar days from the delivery date.
- **Exclusions:** Warranty coverage does not apply to accidental drop damage, liquid spills, unauthorized disassembly, or power surge damage from uncertified transformers.
- **Advance Hardware Replacement:** Enterprise Tier customers enjoy Advance Replacement: replacement equipment is dispatched via Priority Overnight shipping before the damaged unit is returned to our repair depot.
- **Return Material Authorization (RMA):** All hardware returns require an active RMA tracking number issued by the hardware diagnostics desk.

## § 4. Damaged or Lost Shipments Protocols
- **Reporting Window:** Customers must report physical delivery damage or incomplete parcel deliveries within **48 hours** of carrier delivery confirmation.
- **Inspection Documentation:** Customers must photograph parcel exterior, shipping label, internal packing materials, and defective items.
- **Lost in Transit (LIT):** Shipments with no tracking movement updates for **7 consecutive business days** are formally declared Lost in Transit. We dispatch an immediate priority replacement at zero additional charge to the customer.

## § 5. Customer Escalation & Mediation Paths
- **Level 1 (Tier 1 Support Agent):** Frontline ticket intake, triaging, basic troubleshooting, and knowledge base routing.
- **Level 2 (Technical Escalation Specialist):** Senior engineers handling advanced telemetry diagnostics, database errors, and custom API integrations.
- **Level 3 (Engineering & Operations Directorate):** Direct developer intervention for core product defects and critical security patches.
- **Executive Escalation:** If a ticket remains unresolved past 48 hours without status updates, the customer can invoke Executive Escalation by requesting transfer to the Duty Support Manager.
