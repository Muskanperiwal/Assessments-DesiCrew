# Subscription Billing & Account Operations Guide

## § 1. Subscription Tiers & Pricing Model
Our platform provides three flexible subscription tiers:
- **Starter Plan:** $49/month (or $490/year with 2 months free). Supports up to 3 team members, 5,000 monthly API queries, and community support.
- **Professional Plan:** $199/month (or $1,990/year). Supports up to 15 team members, 50,000 monthly API queries, priority email support, and custom webhooks.
- **Enterprise Plan:** Starting at $799/month (annual billing only). Unlimited seats, dedicated infrastructure, 99.95% uptime SLA, 24/7 emergency hotline, and a dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM).

## § 2. Billing Cycles, Renewal & Proration Rules
- **Cycle Timing:** Subscriptions bill in advance on a recurring monthly or annual cadence matching the account creation date.
- **Proration on Mid-Cycle Changes:** Upgrading to a higher tier mid-cycle applies immediate prorated credit for remaining unused days on the previous plan and charges only the fractional difference for the remainder of the month.
- **Auto-Renewal Notice:** Annual plans send automated email notifications 30 calendar days and 7 calendar days prior to scheduled renewal.
- **Grace Period:** Accounts that lapse renewal are maintained in a 14-day grace period with full read-only data access before workspace suspension.

## § 3. Payment Methods & Dunning for Failed Transactions
- **Accepted Payment Forms:** Major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), automated ACH debit (for US annual invoices), and SEPA transfers (for European Union entities).
- **Dunning Process:** If an automated payment fails:
  - **Attempt 1:** Immediate retry notification sent to the billing administrator.
  - **Attempt 2:** System retries transaction after 3 calendar days.
  - **Attempt 3:** System retries transaction after 7 calendar days.
  - **Final Action:** On Day 10 post-failure, workspace features are restricted to billing administration only until card details are updated.

## § 4. Plan Upgrades, Downgrades & Pausing
- **Instant Upgrades:** Upgrades apply immediately; additional seats and higher API quotas are activated within 60 seconds of confirmation.
- **Downgrades:** Downgrading takes effect at the conclusion of the active prepaid billing term to prevent data truncation.
- **Account Pausing:** Customers on annual contracts may pause their subscription for up to **90 calendar days** per calendar year with zero maintenance fees. Stored historical data is preserved intact during the pause duration.

## § 5. Invoicing, Corporate VAT & Tax Receipts
- **Self-Service Invoices:** PDF receipts and tax invoices are automatically generated and downloadable from the Billing Settings dashboard within 2 hours of payment receipt.
- **Tax & VAT Validation:** European companies must input a valid VIES VAT number during checkout to qualify for reverse charge tax exemptions. US entities with tax-exempt non-profit status can submit Form 501(c)(3) to billing@enterprise.com for manual review.
